## BEN

Benin. A 2020 grower survey covered Collines, Borgou, Donga, Zou and Alibori. Orchard rehabilitation at Dassa-Zoumé was recorded by 2022. Consider shea in the north, mango in Donga, Collines and Zou, and oil palm in the southeastern departments of Ouémé and Plateau. TechnoServe combined GPS observations and manual interpretation of high-resolution imagery across about 120 km² of central Benin, including orchards under different management. The national distribution and density study used 2015 and 2019–2021 data. Its maps can be requested from the authors. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/BEN.md) · [World Bank project](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2025/12/04/benin-restoring-old-plantations-to-strengthen-the-cashew-value-chain) · [Open manual labels](https://source.coop/technoserve/cashews-benin) · [National mapping study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425723002468)

## BFA

Burkina Faso. A 2025 sector account lists Cascades, Hauts-Bassins, Sud-Ouest and Centre-Ouest. Shea parkland and open woodland occur in the southwest. Near Dindéresso compare forest and orchard boundaries. Production data describe administrative areas. Inspect the image for each parcel. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/BFA.md)

## CIV

Côte d’Ivoire. Northern production areas named in government sources include Korhogo, Boundiali and Ferkessédougou. The 2022–2023 production records also include Béré, Hambol, Gbêkê, Haut-Sassandra, Marahoué, Gontougo and Bounkani. Cocoa occurs in the southern belt around Soubré, Daloa and San-Pédro. Reviewed southern examples show Cashew adjoining cocoa. On the 10 m BNETD 2020 map, 9 denotes cocoa and 13 denotes Cashew. The ETH cocoa map and FDP cocoa probabilities can guide inspection of the same location. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/CIV.md) · [Government production zones](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Premier%20rapport%20Biennal%20sur%20la%20Transparence%20C%C3%B4te%20d%27Ivoire.pdf) · [BNETD map](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/BNETD_land_cover_v1) · [ETH cocoa map](https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000654400) · [Mixed orchards](dense-cashew-and-cocoa.md)

## GHA

Ghana. TCDA identifies Bono, Bono East and Ahafo as major Cashew regions. Wenchi, Tain, Jaman North and South, and Techiman are useful local names. The former Brong Ahafo boundaries differ from current divisions, and cocoa also occurs there. Consider rubber and oil palm in forest areas, mango in the transition belt and shea in the north. Compare the ETH cocoa map based on 2019–2021 imagery with the local Ghana PlanetMap. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/GHA.md) · [TCDA production regions](https://tcda.gov.gh/tcda-boss-dr-andy-osei-okrah-pushes-for-value-addition-in-cashew-apple-to-boost-economic-growth/) · [COCOBOD](https://cocobod.gh/cocoa-story) · [ETH cocoa map](https://doi.org/10.3929/ethz-b-000654400)

## GIN

Guinea. Earlier sector records mention Kankan and Boké. More local names include Siguiri, Mandiana, Dabola and Kouroussa. Consider cocoa in Forest Guinea and rubber and oil palm in Diecké. The 2021 ALFICC Cashew map covers part of the area adjoining Guinea-Bissau. Check its footprint before use. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/GIN.md) · [ALFICC data](https://github.com/mrp2106/ALFICC)

## GMB

The Gambia. The 2021 national export strategy identifies Cashew development in Western and North Bank. Mango is also reported around Brikama. Examine orchards, mangroves and riverine woodland separately. Use imagery from the relevant period for historical parcels. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/GMB.md)

## GNB

Guinea-Bissau. Orchard surveys record Cashew and mango in Cacheu and Oio. Surveys from 2021–2023 also report Biombo, Bafatá, Gabú, Tombali and Quinara. Coastal landscapes include Cashew, oil palm, mangroves and other trees. ALFICC provides an open 2021 10 m Cashew map covering the country and neighboring areas. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/GNB.md) · [ALFICC repository](https://github.com/mrp2106/ALFICC)

## LBR

Liberia. In 2025 FAO described Cashew as a crop that had recently expanded locally. The project's Cashew evidence is mainly national. Cocoa records exist for Bong, Grand Gedeh and Nimba, and there are named rubber and oil-palm estates. Check nearby place names and crops when a regular, closed canopy is ambiguous. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/LBR.md)

## MLI

Mali. Cashew is recorded in the historical southern Sikasso area. Check Kadiolo, Kolondiéba and Yanfolila. A 2018 project survey observed both Cashew and mango in Sikasso and Yanfolila. Shea also occurs in Yanfolila. Match administrative boundaries to the survey period. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/MLI.md)

## NGA

Nigeria. NEPC certification projects and a 2020 grower survey document Oyo and Kogi. Surveys also cover Kwara, Edo and Enugu. A Landsat Cashew study in Ogbomoso, Oyo, used observations through 2018. CRIN records cocoa at Owena Ayetoro in Ondo and Uhonmora in Edo. Compare original Nigeria PlanetMap Cashew (value 1) with FDP 10 m cocoa probabilities for 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2024. Valid 2024 data were read at three small windows in Ondo, Cross River and Kogi. A research station documents its own site. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/NGA.md) · [NEPC](https://nepc.gov.ng/blog/2023/04/13/298515/) · [Ogbomoso study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10708-023-10861-2) · [FDP cocoa probabilities](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/projects_forestdatapartnership_assets_cocoa_model_2026a)

## SEN

Senegal. Grower surveys or regional production records cover Ziguinchor, Sédhiou, Kolda and Fatick. The ANSD 2016 household and orchard survey provides earlier context. Oil palm occurs in Lower Casamance. Mango and forest occur in Ziguinchor. The project holds a local Senegal PlanetMap. Geo Sénégal provides land-cover vectors for 2010, 2015 and 2020 that can help locate water, fields and woodland. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/SEN.md) · [ANSD orchard survey](https://anads.ansd.sn/index.php/catalog/203) · [Geo Sénégal](https://geosenegal.gouv.sn/-donnees-vectorielles-d-occupation-du-sol-.html)

## SLE

Sierra Leone. In a 2021 survey Cashew growers were concentrated in Kambia, Port Loko and Karene. The survey also covered Bombali and Tonkolili. Cocoa records exist in Kailahun, Kenema and Kono. Compare actual parcels, forest edges and crowns where both crops have regional records. [Country dossier](../../../../cashew_spatial_prior/country_summaries/SLE.md)

## TGO

Togo. The Ministry of Agriculture describes the main Cashew belt from eastern Plateaux to Centrale. Local names include Anié, Est-Mono, Sotouboua, Tchamba and Tchaoudjo. Cashew is also grown in Kara and Savanes. Coffee or cocoa is reported in Agou, Kloto and Amou. In Alibi-I consider natural woodland and shea. The project has a local Togo PlanetMap. An open cropland probability map can help interpret the agricultural setting.
